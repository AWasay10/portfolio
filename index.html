About me

I'm a software engineer who works at the point where development, CRM systems, and automation meet. Most of my work starts with the same question a business owner asks: "why is this still manual?"

I spend my time inside platforms like GoHighLevel, n8n, Make, and Zapier, building the pipelines that move a lead from first contact to booked appointment without anyone chasing it by hand. Some projects are a single CRM cleanup. Others are full systems: a website, a CRM, a set of workflows, and an AI agent, all wired together and talking to each other.

I care less about which tool is trendy and more about whether the system actually holds up once real leads start flowing through it.
